## What this means

- **G2b is crossed.** This Air now matches the public M4 bring-ups past userspace.
  The 2026-09-08 timer/FIQ/`VM_TMR_FIQ_ENA_EL2` wall is behind us for the
  `init`-on-`/init` goal — that wall blocked the `copy_process` WDT walk, and the
  walk got past `copy_process` into `kernel_init` / `do_initcalls`.
- **copy_process is history, and it is not skipped.** `copy_process` is one C
  function in guest Linux (`kernel/fork.c`), the last stop before `kernel_init`
  and `/init`. The WDT walk reached it; the sandboxed `init` process ran. Do not
  rewind the walk or skip `copy_process` — both are banned and were tested to
  fail to unblock.
- This is the same gate `@wtsnz` crossed on 2026-09-06 (shell + userspace on an
  M4 Max). We crossed it a few days later, on an M4 Air, with a real second-macOS
  G1 that the 26.5.1 picker allowed.

## What killed the *next* boot after G2b (fbcon NULL deref)

The follow-up `pp-v0` Image (`998c5a50`) that reached `copy_process` then hit a
**NULL pointer deref in `fbcon_startup+0xd4`** at vaddr `0x190` (load at `+0x190`),
during `simplefb_probe` → `register_framebuffer` → `fbcon_fb_registered`, then
`Kernel panic — Attempted to kill init!`. Not a FIQ miss, not a `copy_process`
failure — it is a framebuffer/console NULL deref *after* we were already past
`copy_process`.

Fix found in the next Image (`aa431d99`, pp-v1): `CONFIG_FB_SIMPLE=n` (skip
`simplefb`/`fbcon`) reaches `copy_process` → `/init` → `G2_INIT_ALIVE` cleanly
(no Oops, no panic). That Image also did not re-boot `aa431d99`.

See `docs/what-came-past-copy-process.md` for the full walk.
